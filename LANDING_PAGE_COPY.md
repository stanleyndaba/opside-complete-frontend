**Shortage variance (delta)**

**Analyzed**

Amazon's receiving record shows **14 units received** against a shipment plan for 17 units.

The three-unit delta survives every receiving record reviewed; it is not a simple posting delay or a duplicate line.

Carrier delivery and warehouse intake records agree on the delivery window, proving the shipment arrived while leaving the shortage inside Amazon's receiving trail.

The subsequent inventory adjustment does not reconcile the three units, and the payout records reviewed do not show that Amazon has already reimbursed them.

This is the distinction a surface-level review misses: the records support a defined shortage, but only **14 of 17 affected units** are presently defensible for filing. Margin keeps the remaining three unresolved rather than overstating the claim.

#### Meta-detail extracted

- **Delta confirmed:** 14 received against 17 planned; the three-unit shortage remains explicit.
- **Shipment identity bound:** FBA shipment, SKU, ASIN, and supplier delivery resolve to one event.
- **Receiving trail aligned:** Carrier delivery and warehouse intake support the same delivery window.
- **Adjustment gap preserved:** The inventory adjustment does not explain or restore the missing units.
- **Reimbursement not found:** No matching payout is recorded for the supported shortage in the reviewed period.

#### The model

##### Why the $179 fixed fee makes sense

You are not paying Margin a percentage of the recovery.

- **Recover Once:** $179 fixed fee
- **Margin commission:** 0%

If Amazon ultimately reimburses $6,240, Margin still takes **0% of that reimbursement**. **You keep the reimbursement.**

The $179 pays for Margin to carry out the defined recovery operation: organize the evidence, prepare the case documentation, manage the response, follow through on Amazon's decision, and check the resulting payout.

Some recovery services charge **15–25% of recovered money** instead.

**Recover Once is different:** You know the cost before approving. You keep 100% of the reimbursement. Margin does not take a percentage of the outcome.

This is not necessarily the cheapest model for every situation. It is designed for sellers who prefer a **defined fixed cost and full ownership of the recovery.**

#### The operation

##### What happens after approval

Once you approve and payment is completed, the **typical first submission target is 1–2 business days after approval**, assuming the available evidence is sufficient to prepare the response.

1. Approved
2. Evidence being prepared
3. Submission ready for your review
4. Submitted after your approval
5. Amazon response received
6. Payout checked
7. Recovered / Partially recovered / Unresolved

Amazon's response time and final decision are outside Margin's control.

#### The boundaries

##### If the evidence or outcome changes

**What if Amazon asks for more evidence?**

If additional evidence is required and Margin can obtain or organize it from the available records, **the operation continues.**

If the required evidence cannot be obtained or the issue cannot be substantiated further, Margin will show the case as **unresolved** and explain why.

**Margin will never represent an unresolved case as recovered.**

**What if Amazon says no?**

Amazon makes the final reimbursement decision. If Amazon rejects the case, Margin records the rejection and the reason available from Amazon.

The result will be shown as **Recovered**—reimbursement confirmed; **Partially recovered**—some reimbursement confirmed; or **Unresolved**—reimbursement not confirmed or the case could not be substantiated.

The $179 remains the fixed fee for carrying out the defined operation, regardless of Amazon's final decision.

#### The finish line

##### Completion

The operation is complete when Margin has prepared and organized the available evidence, prepared the defined recovery response, obtained your approval before submission, submitted the response to Amazon, recorded Amazon's response, checked the resulting payout where the available data permits, and shown the final outcome as Recovered, Partially recovered, or Unresolved.

You will see what happened. The case will not simply disappear into a queue.

## 16 / TRUST / FAQ

### Your questions answered

#### Does the Recovery Audit cost anything?

No. The Recovery Audit is free. You can review what Margin found before deciding whether you want any recovery work managed. No payment is required to run the Audit.

#### What access does Margin need?

Margin uses read-only access or the relevant Amazon report sources for the Audit. You can see the source coverage used for your result.

#### Can Margin submit something without me?

No. Nothing is submitted to Amazon without your explicit approval for that recovery.

#### What happens if Margin does not find anything to handle?

You still receive a clear result: what Margin checked, what appears settled, what may need more evidence or time, and whether there is a sensible next step.

#### How does Recover Once pricing work?

If the Audit identifies a defined recovery Margin can take over, you receive a personalized fixed quote before paid work begins. The scope states what Margin will handle, what you need to approve, and what outcome record you will receive.

#### What if I want Margin to keep looking?

If the Audit shows recurring recovery work that you want off your team’s plate, Recovery Workspace keeps the evidence, cases, responses, and payout tracking together over time.

#### What does payout verified mean?

Margin compares the current supported recovery amount with the relevant Amazon approval and settlement records. Partial payments, reversals, and unresolved balances remain visible rather than being treated as complete.

#### What exactly does Margin handle?

Margin investigates supported opportunities, assembles evidence, prepares approved recovery work, follows responses, and keeps outcomes visible through payout verification.

#### What happens when Amazon rejects something?

The rejection reason remains visible and Margin identifies the next supported path, such as additional evidence, review, appeal, or reassessment.

#### Can I see the evidence?

Yes. Margin keeps the records and supporting evidence behind a finding inspectable so you can understand what happened before approving next steps.

#### Does Margin replace my accounting software?

No. When financial context is needed to establish a recovery, Margin can use relevant supporting records without replacing your accounting system.

#### Can I stop using Margin?

Yes. Margin is designed to keep your recovery records clear and visible, while you remain in control of the work and any approved actions.
