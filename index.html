What Margin does

Every Amazon reimbursement claim has a clock. Margin keeps that clock visible while it organizes invoices, shipment records, Amazon reports, support files, reference IDs, sourcing costs, and payout activity into a reimbursement workflow. Cases move through clear states such as identified, validated, evidence-linked, approved, filed, in-review, reconciled, disputed, blocked, or expired.

Why timing matters

Manual and quarterly reimbursement audits can leave sellers with too little time to act. Lost inventory, damaged units, inbound shortages, refund-without-return activity, fee drift, reimbursement reversals, and payout discrepancies can expire before evidence is ready. Margin connects the claim type, evidence checklist, deadline, approval state, dispute status, and payout trail in one controlled workflow.
