Deadline-aware recovery automation for Amazon sellers

Turn Amazon loss events into claim-ready recoveries before time runs out.

Margin helps Amazon FBA sellers turn loss events, shipment discrepancies, return gaps, fee events, reversals, and payout noise into deadline-aware recovery work. The system detects reimbursement-worthy activity, starts the claim clock, identifies the claim type, matches the required evidence, prepares the case for seller review, and tracks recovery states from detection to payout. Sellers start read-only, review evidence before action, approve before filing, and keep approved recoveries without recovery commissions.

What Margin does

Every Amazon loss event has a clock. Margin keeps that clock visible while it organizes invoices, shipment records, Amazon reports, support files, reference IDs, sourcing costs, and payout activity into a recovery trail. Cases move through clear states such as detected, preparing, claim-ready, blocked, filed, approved, awaiting payout, paid, or re-evaluation. Weak, duplicate, expired, unsupported, or low-confidence findings are held back instead of being pushed into reckless filing volume.

Why timing matters

Manual and quarterly audits can surface recovery issues too late. Lost inventory, damaged units, inbound shortages, refund-without-return activity, fee drift, reimbursement reversals, and payout discrepancies can expire before evidence is ready. Margin is built to move faster than manual review by connecting the event, claim type, evidence checklist, deadline, expected value, approval state, and payout trail in one controlled workflow.

Why sellers trust the workflow

Margin starts read-only, keeps seller approval before filing, and does not take a percentage of approved recoveries. It is a monthly recovery management system for Amazon operators who want stronger evidence, clearer deadlines, and better payout visibility without treating every possible issue as a claim. Amazon makes final reimbursement decisions; Margin prepares, organizes, and tracks evidence-backed recovery work.
